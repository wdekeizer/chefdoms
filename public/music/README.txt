YOUR OWN MUSIC
==============
Drop audio files in this folder (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, flac or opus) and Chefdoms
plays them instead of its built-in soundtrack. No restart needed: players just
refresh the page.

The file name decides when a track plays:

  lobby...     e.g. lobby-theme.mp3      in the lobby
  battle...    e.g. battle-01.ogg        while you are fighting
  anything else                          during normal play (shuffled)

A category with no files keeps the built-in music, so you can replace only the
battle music, for example. Everyone connected to your server hears your files.
